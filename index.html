Parent-Child Concerns and Opinions on AI-Based Collaborative Supervision Systems

Investigating how parents understand and manage their children's online video consumption, and what kinds of support or tools could help — through interviews with parent-child pairs about viewing habits, involvement, and attitudes toward AI features that summarize or mediate what kids watch.
